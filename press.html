Full bio

Rooney Pitchford writes country songs with a poet’s ear. Raised in the Bay Area and trained in poetry at Stanford, he came up playing along to John Prine, Neil Young and Townes Van Zandt records before settling in Austin, and his sound carries their mix of plainspoken storytelling and electric rock ’n’ roll.

His crowdfunded debut album, Familiar Places (2016), was written and produced in Austin, and he spent the year after its release crisscrossing the country to play it. The EP Pachoncito (2017), the single Peninsula (2018) and the EP Red-Tailed Hawk (2022) followed. His newest, the six-song EP Turnaround Copy, arrived April 1, 2026.

On stage he moves between solo fingerpicking and a full band. He has played Sofar Sounds San Francisco, the songwriters’ circle at Cheatham Street Warehouse in San Marcos, and a full-band homecoming show at The Milk Bar on Haight Street.
